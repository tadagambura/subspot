# SubSpot

**Every subscription, renewal and free trial in one place**

> #14 of 33 · Wave 2: Recurring keepers · Used: Monthly · Easy · Revenue potential: Medium–high · Money & Work

## What it is

List your subscriptions (streaming, gym, apps, phone contract, insurance) and SubSpot shows the real monthly and yearly total, warns you before renewals and free-trial endings, and keeps a 'cancel before renewal' list.

## Why it's in the portfolio

People hate wasting money on forgotten subscriptions, so the hook is emotional and the value is easy to prove ('you'd save £26 a month'). Renewals keep arriving, so reminders keep bringing users back.

## Who it's for

Anyone with several subscriptions.

**The problem:** Subscriptions renew quietly, free trials convert to paid, and nobody knows their real monthly total.

## Version one

- Quick add with common services pre-filled
- Monthly, yearly and per-use cost
- Renewal and trial-ending reminders
- 'Cancel before renewal' list
- Total recurring spend dashboard

**Example:** A user adds everything, sees £87 a month, and cancels two services before they renew.

## Later

- Shared household subscriptions
- Yearly recap card
